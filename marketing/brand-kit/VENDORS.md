Brand colors: Charcoal `#121110`, Ember `#F07A32`, Bone `#F3EDE4`, Gold (price only) `#F6C56F`. Fonts: Archivo (display, expanded), Geist (text).

Magnet size note: the artwork is 24x12 in (2:1), which matches Vistaprint's 12x24 option. For a 12x18 magnet, ask Claude/Dan to re-render at 18x12 (the template takes any size) rather than letting the vendor stretch it.

Embroidery note: vendors re-digitize the logo for thread. The fine brick lines in the chimney may be simplified at beanie size. Ask for a digital proof before approving.

Order magnets and cards first; they generate calls. Apparel can follow once crew size is known.
