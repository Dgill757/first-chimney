How the website talks to your CRM

No copy and paste, ever. Every form on the site feeds the CRM the moment someone hits Send.

1. VisitorFills out a form, books a time, designs a fireplace or takes the health check.
2. Your siteChecks the form, blocks spam, and sends it securely to the CRM.
3. CRM recordCreates or updates the contact, fills the custom fields, adds tags and a note.
4. PipelineOpens the right opportunity in the right stage (Jobs, Fireplace Projects, Partners or Recruiting).
5. AutomationThe tag starts a workflow: alert to you, reply to them, follow ups.

If the same person fills out another form later, the system finds them by email or phone and updates the same contact instead of making a duplicate. It also re-fires the automations so a repeat customer is treated like a fresh lead.

Your 31 automations

All 31 are built, published and switched on. This is what each one does, what starts it, and what you do. Search or filter by group.

Rules every automation follows

  • Nobody who replied “STOP” or is tagged Do Not Contact gets another message.
  • Marketing texts only go to people who ticked the SMS consent box on the website.
  • If a customer replies, the follow up sequence for them stops, and you get an alert.
  • Reviews are requested from every completed customer, never only the happy ones, and nothing is ever offered in exchange for a review. That keeps you inside Google and FTC rules.
  • New lead and customer reply alerts reach you by email today. Text alerts and all customer texts start once your phone number is approved.

In the app or on the website, open Settings → My Profile → Notifications. For each type, choose the channels you want: in app, email, mobile (push) and text. Turn on every option for inbound messages, appointments, tasks and reviews. These use your phone's push alerts, which are the fastest.

If something looks wrong

Most problems have a two minute answer.

I did not get an alert for a new lead.

Check spam, and make sure the notification settings in Your phone are on. Then open Contacts and search the customer's name or filter by the tag Website Form. If the contact is there, the website worked and only the alert is missing. Tell Dan.

A customer says they never got a text.

Texts do not send until your business number is registered. See Work with Dan. Emails do send.

The booking page shows no times.

Your hours may need updating, or the day is full. Tell Dan your real hours and he fixes the calendar.

I moved a card by mistake.

Drag it back. If it triggered a message (for example Job Complete sent a review text), no harm done, but let the customer know if it was confusing.

How do I stop messages to one person?

Open the contact and add the tag Do Not Contact. That blocks every channel and removes them from all automations.

How do I add a customer by hand?

Contacts → Add Contact. Add their name, phone and email, then open Opportunities → Jobs and add an opportunity for them. If you tag them Website Lead, the new lead automation will run for them as well.

Can I edit an automation?

You can open and read every one. Please ask Dan to change wording or timing so nothing breaks. It takes him a few minutes.

Where is my Owner's Operating Manual?

The 31 page manual and 13 page Team Handbook come with this guide as PDFs. The manual covers licensing by state, insurance, scripts, pricing, hiring, canvassing, networking dates and a 30 day launch plan.

Something is broken or I want a change.

Text or email Dan with a screenshot and what you expected. Include the website reference number (it looks like FC-8XQUWAI) if it is about a form.
